Signature gradient

Fuchsia #ed26f2 → #e85be4 → #efa9e6 → #f7d9f3 at 96°. Reserved for the "SOCIAL" wordmark and one hero moment per asset. Use one bold accent per campaign; navy carries the authority.

Body — Hanken Grotesk

Social Summit brings real estate professionals together around useful ideas, personal growth and the people sitting next to them. Expect sharp speakers, practical workshops and plenty of time to meet each other.

Bold for emphasis. Sentence case, leading 1.6.

Usage

  • ✓Set Horizon in uppercase with tight tracking (−0.02em) and leading around 0.9 for headlines, city names and single hero words.
  • ✓Use Hanken Grotesk for body, UI, labels and supporting copy.
  • ✗Don't set Horizon in lowercase, for body copy, or for anything longer than a short headline.
