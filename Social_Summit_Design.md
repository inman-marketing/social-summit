# Social Summit — design.md

Social Summit, founded by Andrew Jevin and powered by Inman. Social Summit is the dominant identity; "Powered by Inman" is a secondary endorsement.

## Color
- **Fuchsia** (Primary) — `#ed26f2`
- **Pink** (Gradient mid) — `#e567db`
- **Soft Pink** (Gradient tail) — `#eda9e4`
- **Azure** (Script accent) — `#3e8ffa`
- **Inman Blue** (Endorsement) — `#0b84ff`
- **Navy** (Foundation) — `#161a48`
- **Magenta** — `#e948e6`
- **Champagne** (Warm sections) — `#f4efe7`
- **Deep Navy** (Backgrounds) — `#0e1234`
- **Signature gradient** — `linear-gradient(96deg, #ED26F2 0%, #E85BE4 42%, #EFA9E6 74%, #F7D9F3 100%)`

One bold accent per campaign (fuchsia or Inman blue). Navy carries authority.

## Type
- **Display — Horizon** 800/900, UPPERCASE, tracking −0.02em, leading 0.9–1.0.
- **Script — Mistrully** one or two words, usually azure #3e8ffa. Never body.
- **Body / UI — Hanken Grotesk** 400–800, sentence case, leading 1.6.
- **Editorial — Newsreader** pull quotes, testimonials, Inman-facing material.

```css
--font-display: 'Horizon', 'Poppins', sans-serif;
--font-script:  'Mistrully', 'Caveat', cursive;
--font-sans:    'Hanken Grotesk', system-ui, sans-serif;
--font-serif:   'Newsreader', Georgia, serif;
```
